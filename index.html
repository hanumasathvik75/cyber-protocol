How does SafeMind block Incognito completely?

Unlike regular extensions that get turned off in Incognito, SafeMind writes directly to the Windows Registry Group Policy (HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Google\Chrome) setting IncognitoModeAvailability = 1. This removes the Incognito option from Chrome entirely so kids cannot open it.

Why is On-Device AI better than traditional parental controls?

Traditional apps rely on basic keyword lists that block words like "dirty carpet" (false alarms) while missing phrases like "steamy bedroom scenes uncensored" (false negatives). SafeMind runs a Sentence Transformer locally in sub-15ms RAM to understand the real human intent.
